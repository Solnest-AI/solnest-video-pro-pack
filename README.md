# Video Pro Pack, by Solnest AI

Nine video skills for Claude Code, installed in one go. Make avatar videos and dub them into other languages with HeyGen, build animated explainers, comparison videos and 3D captions with HyperFrames, and get film-style prompts for Seedance.

You describe the video in plain words. Claude does the rest.

## What is inside

| Skill | What you can ask for |
|---|---|
| HeyGen avatar | "Create my avatar" (a reusable face and voice) |
| HeyGen video | "Make a 30 second video of my avatar saying this" |
| HeyGen translate | "Translate this video into Spanish" |
| Explainer | "Make a 60 second explainer on why every rental has a lockbox" |
| Duo | "Put Airbnb on the left screen and Vrbo on the right" (foldable phone video) |
| 3D captions | "Add 3D captions around me in this clip" |
| Session story | "Make a film of a session with me" |
| Day in my life | "Make a day in my life as my agent" |
| Seedance cinematic | "Write a cinematic Seedance prompt for a drone shot of my rental" |

## What you need

- The Claude Code desktop app (or Claude Code in a terminal), on a Mac or Windows computer.
- For the three HeyGen skills: a HeyGen account. Videos, avatars and translations use your HeyGen plan or credits.
- For the five animation skills: Node.js 22 or newer and ffmpeg. Claude checks for both and offers to install them. HyperFrames itself is free.
- For the Seedance prompts: nothing to write them. To turn a prompt into a clip you need a Higgsfield account, and clips cost credits there.
- Cost of this pack: free.

You do not need all of it. Skills you have no account for just sit there until you do.

## Set it up in 3 steps

1. **Copy this page's link.**
2. **Paste it into Claude Code and say "set this up".** Claude installs everything and tells you what worked. Quit and reopen Claude Code once when it tells you to.
3. **Try it:** say "make a 15 second foldable phone video comparing two apps, left screen and right screen".

## Good to know

- **Updates install themselves.** Once a day, when Claude Code starts, the pack quietly moves to the newest release. You never have to ask.
- **An update never wipes your changes.** Your avatars, your video projects, your sign-ins and any change you asked Claude to make stay exactly as they are.
- **Want it to work differently?** Just tell Claude ("always make my videos vertical", "use my brand colors"). It remembers.
- **Claude asks before spending.** Anything that uses HeyGen or Higgsfield credits waits for your yes.
- **Already have one of these skills?** If it came from another installer, the pack leaves your copy alone and tells you.
